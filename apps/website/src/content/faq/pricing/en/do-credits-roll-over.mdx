---
question: "Do unused credits roll over?"
order: 3
---

Plan credits reset at the end of each billing cycle and don't roll over — monthly on monthly plans, and once a year on annual plans, which receive their whole year of credits up front. **Top-up credits do persist.** They're valid for a year and aren't affected by your plan's reset. Credits work on Comfy Cloud, and on Comfy Desktop **only when calling Partner Nodes.** Comfy Desktop itself is free.
