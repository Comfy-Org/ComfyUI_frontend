---
question: "How does Comfy Cloud pricing actually work?"
order: 0
---

Every plan includes a pool of **credits** — refilled each month on monthly plans, granted for the whole year up front on annual plans. Credits are spent on three things: **active GPU time** while a workflow is running, **Partner Nodes** (proprietary models like Nano Banana Pro), and **[Comfy Agent](/agent)** usage. You're never charged for idle time. Building or editing a workflow yourself costs nothing. You only spend while a job is actually running or the agent is working.
