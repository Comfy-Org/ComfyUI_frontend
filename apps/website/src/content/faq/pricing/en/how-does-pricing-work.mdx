---
question: "How does Comfy Cloud pricing actually work?"
order: 0
---

Every plan includes a pool of **credits** — refilled each month on monthly plans, granted for the whole year up front on annual plans. Credits are spent on two things: **active GPU time** while a workflow is running, and **Partner Nodes** (proprietary models like Nano Banana Pro). You're never charged for idle time. Building or editing a workflow costs nothing. You only spend while a job is actually running.
